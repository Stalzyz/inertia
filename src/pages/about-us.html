M - Tel Networks aims at offering high-quality Internet and Intranet on Wireless Technology. Internet, today is the basic need for communication, information, and education. It has wide acceptance in our society. The technology that we have implemented provides a high-speed and cost-effective internet connection to people.

M - Tel Networks is one of the best Internet Service Providers operating in this region and the client base that we have and their satisfaction level will declare for the best wireless technology that we provide at an affordable cost. We offer high-quality, high speed, low cost and easy to use Internet connection for home-based users, small entrepreneurs, corporate users, and educational institutions, granting clients to explore the true potential of the Internet. Our services are flexible and scalable to keep pace with increasing Internet needs. We provide WI-FI connections for all users as per their requirements without any compromise in quality. A site survey will be done by technical experts in the scheduled area where the connection is required and the work will be done without any delay and disturbance to the occupants. Once the connection is provided the subscriber will have access to the internet with excellent speed and will feel the latest internet technology with crystal clear clarity.

By using the latest broadband technology called 'fixed wireless', the transceiver will be mounted on the roof of the client's building and the transceiver will be positioned to the closest Base Station which will send and receive signals. The signal received by the subscriber-unit is sent through a wired cable to the building to reach either the computer or the network-switch in the office or institution.

Security is required to protect your systems, accounts and banking details, etc. M - Tel Networks provides a secured internet service to its users, which is perfectly maintained by professionals. M - Tel internet service has enough security for any external and internal threats. Our users need not worry while using bank details and accessing any confidential information over the web because of the strong security system provided to them.
