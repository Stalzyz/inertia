Privacy Policy

Information we collect

When you use the contact form, we receive the name, email address, phone number and message you enter. The sign-in form collects the username and password you provide. The sign-up form collects your name, email address and password. A short-lived captcha cookie is set when the contact form is used, and embedded map content may set cookies through Google.

How we use it

Contact details are used to reply to your enquiry. Account details are used to sign you in or create your account. The captcha cookie helps confirm that a contact form submission came from a person. We use this information only for the purpose it was given for.

Sharing

We do not sell the details you submit. Embedded map content is served by Google, so that content is subject to Google's own policies. Our Cookies Policy explains the cookies involved.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or remove the details you submitted. See our Data Deletion page for how to make that request.
