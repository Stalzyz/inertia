Selected Work

Leased Line · LAN / WAN · Intranet / VPN

Always-on connectivity for a multi-office team

One dedicated circuit. Offices joined into one network. A monthly cost fixed at the start.

A normal dial-up connection is not always active. It shares the line, and the quality is hard to promise. When two offices need to work as one network, that uncertainty gets in the way.

  • Specified the leased line between the endpoints.
  • Set distance and speed; that fixed the monthly fee.
  • Joined the offices with LAN / WAN and secured remote access with VPN.

The connection stays active. It carries no other communications, so the carrier can assure the promised quality. Internal systems stay behind a firewall, and authorized users reach them through encryption.

Distance and speed set the fee.
