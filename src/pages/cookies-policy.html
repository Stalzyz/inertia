Cookies Policy

Cookies used on this site

The contact form sets a captcha cookie named mtel_captcha. It holds the characters shown in the captcha image so that a submission can be checked. The cookie is set with HTTP-only and SameSite protection, and the captcha response is not stored for reuse.

Embedded content

Pages that show a Google Maps iframe load content from Google. That embedded content may set its own cookies, which are controlled by Google rather than by this website.

Cookies we do not add

This website does not add advertising or analytics cookies. Site search, navigation and page content run locally without tracking cookies.

Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the captcha cookie may prevent the contact form from being submitted, in which case please use the phone or email details below.
